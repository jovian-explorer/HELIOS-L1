Heliophysical Turbulence & Energy Cascade Physics:
Kolmogorov (1941) Inertial Subrange (f⁻⁵/³)

Governs local isotropic fluid turbulence where kinetic energy cascades conservatively from large scale solar eddies down toward kinetic dissipation scales without net loss.

Iroshnikov-Kraichnan (f⁻³/²)

Describes magnetohydrodynamic turbulence dominated by counter-propagating Alfvén wave packet collisions along the background interplanetary magnetic field B₀.

Taylor Frozen-in-Flow Hypothesis (λ = v_sw / f)

Because solar wind super-Alfvénic speed (~450 km/s) far exceeds eddy turnover speed, temporal frequencies measured at L1 directly map to spatial structures advected past the spacecraft.
